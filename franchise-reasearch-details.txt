# Executive Summary  
India’s home services sector – encompassing laundry, car wash, and residential cleaning – is booming. Urbanisation, rising incomes and dual-income households are driving sustained growth. Industry analyses estimate India’s total laundry and dry-cleaning market at **₹30,000+ crore (~USD 36B)** (2020), growing ~5–10% annually.  The **organized (franchise-style) laundry segment** is still nascent (≈USD 0.74B in 2024) but growing faster.  Similarly, the **residential cleaning market** was about **USD 3.8–14.5B** (depending on scope) in the mid‑2020s, with ~7%+ CAGR.  The **car wash segment** (professional vehicle cleaning) is also expanding (≈USD 0.79B in 2024) as Indians buy more vehicles and seek convenience.  

Clean7’s franchise offers investors a front-row seat in these tailwinds.  Our model leverages tech-enabled booking, premium service quality, and recurring demand.  Early benchmarks show **healthy unit economics** (e.g. ₹20–175K monthly profit per laundry outlet) and **break-even often within 1–1.5 years** on moderate investments.  We present below a detailed market analysis (with data and citations) followed by our franchise offering (ROI models, costs, support) to help investors evaluate this opportunity.  

## Market Size & Growth  

- **Laundry Services:**  India’s overall laundry/dry-cleaning industry was ~USD 36B in 2020.  Organized laundry (tech-enabled pick-up/drop-off) is much smaller (~USD 0.74B in 2024) but projected ~5.2% CAGR (to USD 1.0B by 2030).  Studies estimate ~₹30,000+ crore in 2024 (≈USD 3.6B) with **8–10% growth**.  Organized chains are growing faster than the overall sector because they solve “last-mile” pain points (pickup, quality, tracking).  Penetration is still low – only ~4–5% organized – so Clean7 has room to expand.  
- **Residential Cleaning (House Help):**  The Indian home cleaning market (including maid services, deep-cleaning, etc.) was valued at roughly **USD 3.8B (₹30,000+ crore) in 2024**, with ~7.3% CAGR to 2032.  Another estimate covering broader “cleaning services” (residential+commercial) puts it at USD 14.5B in 2025, rising to USD 25.9B by 2034 (6.45% CAGR).  Urban Metro regions (Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru) lead adoption.  Key drivers: rising dual-income families, hygiene awareness post-2020, and convenience preference.  **Figure:** Residential cleaning market is forecast to jump from $3.8B in 2024 to $6.9B by 2032, reflecting strong demand (full forecast chart below).

- **Car Wash Services:**  The professional car wash market in India is still emerging.  One analysis values it at **USD 790M (≈₹6,600 crore) in 2024**.  Growth drivers include India’s expanding vehicle base (~295 million registered vehicles by 2022) and urban customers shifting from manual to professional washes (an estimated 72% of metro car owners now use paid services).  Monsoon season slows demand (rain reduces need for washes), while summer/dry seasons peak.  Regulatory push for water recycling (CPCB rules) is both a cost factor and opportunity (eco-friendly differentiation).  

## Demand Drivers & Customer Profile  
- **Urban Dual-Income Families:**  Demand is highest in Tier-1/Tier-2 cities (Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Chennai) where time-poor professionals (25–45 age group) outsource chores.  By 2036, India’s urban population will be ~600M, boosting service demand.  
- **Women’s Time Poverty:**  Indian women (15–59) average ~305 minutes/day on unpaid household work vs. 75 minutes by men.  As more women work professionally, paying for laundry/cleaning relieves a heavy burden.  
- **Hygiene & Convenience:**  COVID-19 heightened hygiene focus.  Consumers increasingly value guaranteed 48–72hr laundry turnaround and professional cleaning quality.  Tech adoption (apps, QR tracking) also appeals to younger demographics.  
- **Seasonality:**  **Laundry** demand is fairly stable year-round, with slight upticks pre-festivals. **Cleaning services** see modest spikes before major festivals or school holidays. **Car wash** peaks in dry months (Oct–May) and dips during Jun–Sept rains.  

## Pricing Benchmarks  
- **Laundry (Wash & Fold):** ~₹50–100 per kg (basic wash/iron).  Premium services (delicates, dry-clean) cost more.  For example, popular providers charge ~₹70–99/kg for wash/iron.  A typical first-time laundry order (~8–10 kg) yields ₹500–800.  
- **Car Wash:** Exterior wash ₹300–500 for a sedan; full wash+interior ≤₹800.  Daily mobile/detergent costs are modest (~₹50–100 per car).  E.g. one operator notes “30–40 cars/day × ₹300 each → ₹90k–120k revenue/month”.  Thus monthly gross ~₹80k–100k in Tier-1 (4–7 cars/day).  
- **House Cleaning:** General cleaning of a 2–3BHK starts ~₹1,500 per visit; deep cleaning ₹3,000–5,000+.  Many customers book **fortnightly or monthly**.  Corporate housekeeping (offices/societies) commands higher rates (budget-dependent).  

## Unit Economics & KPIs  
- **Average Order Value (AOV):**  Laundry: ~₹600 per order (8–10 kg); Car Wash: ~₹300 per wash; Cleaning: ~₹1,500 per job (2–3BHK).  
- **Frequency:**  Laundry: ~3–4 orders/month (weekly service); Car Wash: ~4–8/month (weekly or bi-weekly); Cleaning: ~1/month or on-demand.  
- **Gross Margins:**  Laundry franchises report **20–35% profit margins** net; gross margins (after supplies/labour but before rent/admin) are ~60%.  Car wash margins are high (~65–70%), since water and chemicals are low-cost.  Cleaning services (labour intensive) have gross margins ~50–60%.  
- **Customer Acquisition (CAC):**  Digital marketing (SEM/SEO/Facebook) and promotions typically spend ₹300–₹500 per new customer.  Referral incentives (e.g. free pickup) help.  Early estimates: CAC ~1–2k leads per retained customer.  
- **Customer Lifetime Value (LTV):**  With high retention, a single loyal laundry customer (~₹2,500 revenue/month) can generate **₹50–60k+** over 2–3 years.  Similarly, monthly car wash (₹1200/month) yields ~₹25–30k LTV; cleaning customers may spend ₹18–30k/year.  (Given repeat needs, LTV often exceeds initial customer acquisition cost by 10× or more.)  
- **Traffic & Conversion:**  Digital businesses in this sector see ~5–10% conversion from app sign-ups to first order.  Maintaining app rating ≥4.5 and fast service are critical.

## Startup & Operating Costs  
- **Franchise Fee:** ₹2–5 lakh (one-time) for brand/license, training, and tech platform (assumed).  
- **Equipment:**  Key assets include commercial washers/dryers (~₹150k–250k each), ironing/press machines (₹50k+), cleaning vacuum/steamers (₹10–20k), car-wash gear (pressure washer ₹15k, vans/bikes ₹50k).  A small laundry unit (2 washers + 1 dryer) costs ~₹8–10L.  
- **Space & Fit-Out:**  Rental deposit + advance (~₹3–4 lakh for Tier-1 space, less in smaller cities).  Renovation/signage ~₹2L.  
- **Technology:**  POS/billing hardware ~₹30k; CRM/booking software (if not provided franchise) ~₹10–20k annual.  
- **Working Capital:**  Detergents/chemicals inventory (₹20–50k stock), collections cycles (typically <30 days).  Initial marketing budget ~₹50k for launch promos.  

**Operating Expenses (monthly):**  For a Tier-2 outlet: rent ₹30k, utilities ₹5k, salaries (4–5 staff at ₹8–10k each) ~₹40k, marketing ₹5k, misc ₹5k.  Total ~₹85k–100k.  In a Tier-1 city, these double (e.g. rent ₹1L, staff ₹80k+, etc).  Fixed+variable costs run ~30–40% of revenue at scale.

## Competitive Landscape  
India’s home-services market is **highly fragmented**.  A few organized chains coexist with thousands of small businesses. Key players include:

- **Laundry:** Major chains like **UClean** (350+ outlets), **DhobiLite**, **WashXpress**, **The Laundry Basket**, **Pick My Laundry**, etc.  The organized segment is only ~3–5% of the total laundry market, so national brands have strong room to grow.  Many local “dhobi” shops remain unbranded; Clean7’s focus on digital booking and quality differentiates from these.  
- **Car Wash:** Brands such as **Express Car Wash**, **3M Car Care**, **The Car Laundry**, **Auto Spa** (from Mumbai), as well as tech platforms like **Ola Car Wash** and local mobile-wash startups.  Most organized car washes concentrate in Tier-1 cities; the rest of the market is “unorganized” (manual washers by the roadside).  Water-recycling regulations are pushing professionalism.  
- **House Cleaning:** The leader is **Urban Company** (formerly UrbanClap) with a pan-India on-demand model; others like **Housejoy**/Helpr, **Clean India Services**, **Favorz** also compete.  Many small agencies or freelancers dominate mid-market.  According to reports, *top players (Urban Company, Clean India, Favorz, etc.) focus on tech platforms and standardized training to capture market share*.  

Clean7’s multi-service model (laundry + car wash + cleaning) is relatively unique. By bundling these high-frequency needs under one brand and tech platform, Clean7 can cross-sell across services and build stronger customer relationships.

## Regulatory & Operational Considerations  
- **GST / Taxes:**  Home services generally attract GST.  Laundry and car wash services are taxed at **18% (GST)**.  Housekeeping/cleaning services can be taxed lower (some domestic cleaning qualifies for 5% GST under certain schemes), but in practice most commercial housekeeping is 5–18%.  Franchisees must register for GST and comply with invoicing.  
- **Licensing:**  No special permits beyond normal business registration are needed for laundry/car wash.  Car washes must comply with local pollution control (e.g. water recycling mandates).  House help businesses should follow labour laws (minimum wages, social security for staff).  
- **Labour:**  Labour costs are moderate.  A full-time laundry/helper in Mumbai/Bangalore typically earns **₹4k–8k/month**.  Skilled car-wash attendants ~₹10k.  Domestic cleaners ~₹6k–10k.  High staff turnover is a risk; franchise training programs and fair wages (≥minimum rates) are needed.  
- **Supply Chain & Logistics:**  Essential supplies (detergents, chemicals, equipment parts) are readily available via national distributors.  Fuel/logistics: delivery riders (scooter) cost ~₹50–70k each.  Traffic congestion can impact pickups/deliveries in big cities; route optimization (via app) mitigates delays.  Water scarcity/regulations necessitate in-house recycling systems (capital cost upfront, but reduces ongoing water cost).  
- **Technology:**  A reliable tech platform (app/website for orders, CRM for ops, digital payments) is critical.  Franchisees need basic connectivity and smartphone(s).  Most operators leverage cloud-based systems (included in franchise support).  

## Risks & Mitigations  
- **Competition and Price Sensitivity:**  Customers may balk at organized prices if cheaper locals exist.  *Mitigation:* Emphasize quality, reliability, and service guarantees. Build loyalty with subscription packages and app-based convenience.  
- **Seasonality:**  Car wash demand drops in monsoon. *Mitigate* by cross-selling other services (laundry or home cleaning) or offering monsoon-specific deals (e.g. interiors + spray).  
- **Staff Attrition:**  Service businesses face high churn. *Mitigate* by providing training, incentives, and career path (clean7 can certify staff to reduce turn).  
- **Operational Complexity:**  Running 3 services can strain resources. *Mitigate* with efficient scheduling and clear SOPs. Franchisees typically start with 1–2 services and scale up.  
- **Economic Fluctuations:**  Discretionary services can dip in downturns. *Mitigate* by balancing essential (laundry) vs discretionary (detailing) offerings, and by offering budget-friendly packages.
